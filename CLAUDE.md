# Toolshed

Static site at https://toolshed.ffff0h.com (GitHub Pages, repo `zohaibhamdulay/toolshed`). Styled to match the portfolio at `Desktop/Portfolio/site`.

- **Adding or changing a tool:** follow [ADDING-TOOLS.md](ADDING-TOOLS.md). Always do its privacy-policy review (step 3) and update [privacy/index.html](privacy/index.html), including the "Last updated" date, whenever a tool's data handling differs from what the policy says.
- Tools are domain-specific, not generic utilities. Don't put that wording on the site.
- Every page carries the AdSense `<script>` in `<head>`; keep it on new pages.
