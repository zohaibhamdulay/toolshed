Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version: the tools on Toolshed run on your own device. Files, images, text and passwords you use with them are not uploaded or stored by us. The site shows ads from Google, which uses cookies.

This policy covers Toolshed at toolshed.ffff0h.com and the tools linked from it on ffff0h.com subdomains (for example, the Image Steganography Suite at steganography.ffff0h.com). Toolshed is run by Zohaib Hamdule ("I", "me").

What the tools do with your data

Every tool currently on Toolshed processes your data locally, in your browser or on your computer.

  • Files, images, messages and passwords you give a tool stay on your device. They are not sent to me or to any server I run.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • I do not store, sell or share anything you put into the tools.

For example, the Image Steganography Suite hides and extracts data in images on your device. The image, the hidden data and any password you use are never uploaded.

Advertising and cookies

Toolshed uses Google AdSense to show ads. Because of this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other services the site relies on

Like any website, loading these pages involves a few third parties. They may see technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for the ffff0h.com domain. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on the pages. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

I do not use analytics or tracking scripts of my own, and I do not set any cookies myself.

Future tools

New tools may need to work differently, for example by processing something on a server. If a tool ever collects, uploads or stores your data, that tool's page will say so clearly before you use it, and this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Children

Toolshed is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy
